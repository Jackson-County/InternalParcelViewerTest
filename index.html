Learn what this experimental 3D Parcel Viewer is doing.
This 3D Parcel Viewer is an experimental research project by the Jackson County GIS team.

In many parts of Jackson County, condominium and vertical subdivision parcels are stacked on top of each other in the same footprint. On a traditional 2D map this can look like “spaghetti” — overlapping lines and polygons that are hard to interpret.

This application uses a 3D scene to separate those stacked parcels into floors and volumes so you can visually understand how units relate to each other, to the building, and to the ground. Because this is 3D, it relies heavily on your device’s graphics hardware and network connection.

When you filter buildings, floors, or parcels, the scene may take a few seconds to update while it loads and redraws features. On some devices this can appear as brief pauses, delayed labels, or lower-quality graphics while the scene catches up.

The Jackson County GIS Program is actively tuning this experience to balance detail and performance. If something seems slow or doesn’t draw as expected, it may be related to those 3D and hardware limitations rather than the parcel data itself.

This viewer is provided as a research and visualization tool . For official records or legal questions, please refer to the Jackson County Assessment Department and recorded documents.
